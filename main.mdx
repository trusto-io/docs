# Operate Trusto chargeback prevention

Trusto helps merchants, payment service providers, and acquirers act on chargeback signals before they become completed disputes. The platform receives alerts from payment-network providers, connects them with enrolled merchant and transaction data, and gives your team one place to record the action taken.

Use this documentation to run the alert workflow or connect your own systems to it.

## How the workflow operates

| Stage | What happens | Your action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Dispute signal** | A cardholder contacts their issuer about a transaction or suspected fraud. | No action is required until Trusto creates an alert. |
| **2. Detection** | A prevention provider detects the dispute signal and sends it to Trusto. | Monitor for new alerts in the portal or through the API. |
| **3. Match** | Trusto associates the alert with enrolled merchant data and, when available, an external transaction. | Verify the match using transaction identifiers, card details, date, amount, and currency. |
| **4. Resolve** | You refund the transaction, decline to refund it, or identify why the alert is invalid. | Complete the payment action first, then record the matching resolution in Trusto. |
| **5. Confirm** | Trusto sends the outcome to the alert provider and updates the alert status. | Verify that the outcome succeeded and retain supporting references in the comments. |

## Start with your role

### Alert operations

Use [Matching and resolving alerts](/alerts) if you work alerts in the Trusto portal. It explains how to:

- match an alert to the correct transaction;
- prioritize alerts before they expire;
- verify an existing refund or chargeback;
- refund the full alert amount and confirm that the refund succeeded;
- choose **Refunded**, **Not Refunded**, or the correct **Invalid** reason;
- record evidence that another operator can review later.

### API integration

Use the [Trusto API reference](/api-reference/trusto-api) if you retrieve or resolve alerts from an ERP, payment platform, or internal operations tool. It documents:

- API-key authentication with `X-API-KEY`;
- alert list filters and pagination;
- alert-detail and outcome payloads;
- resolution requests and response statuses;
- supported webhook events.

## Daily operating checklist

1. Review new and unresolved alerts frequently.
2. Prioritize alerts approaching their expiration time.
3. Match each alert to one payment before taking action.
4. Check whether the payment was already refunded or charged back.
5. If refunding, process the exact alert amount and confirm the refund succeeded.
6. Record the resolution in Trusto with useful references in the comments.
7. Verify that the alert status and outcome reflect what happened.

Trusto recommends completing the alert action and recording its outcome within 24 hours. Acting quickly gives the refund notification time to reach the issuer, but a refund does not guarantee that a chargeback will be prevented.

## Resolution decisions

| Decision | Use it when | Resulting alert status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Refunded** | The correct transaction was refunded successfully. | `RESOLVED` |
| **Not Refunded** | You chose not to refund and are prepared to continue through the chargeback process. | `CHARGEBACK` |
| **Invalid** | The transaction was previously refunded, a chargeback was already received, the alert is a duplicate, or the transaction cannot be found. | `INVALID` |

For the evidence to collect for each invalid reason, see [Choose the resolution status](/alerts#choose-the-resolution-status). For exact API values and payload examples, see [Provide an alert resolution](/api-reference/trusto-api#provide-an-alert-resolution).

## Before going live

- Confirm that merchant descriptors and identifiers are enrolled correctly.
- Decide who owns alert monitoring, transaction matching, refunds, and exception review.
- Give operators access to the payment records needed for matching.
- Generate and store API credentials securely if you are integrating programmatically.
- Test alert retrieval and resolution in your integration before using it for live operations.
